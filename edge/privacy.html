Community Swim — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Community Swim is a browser extension that shows and lets you post time-stamped comments while you watch shows on Hulu and Netflix. This page explains what data it handles.

What the extension collects

  • Comments you post: the text, the timestamp in the episode, the episode's identifier (the id in the player page URL, e.g. hulu-…), and the time you posted.
  • Likes you give to comments.
  • A display name you choose (defaults to viewer-xxxx) and a random anonymous identifier generated on your device. The identifier lets you delete your own comments and prevents double-counting likes. It is not linked to your Hulu, Netflix, or Google account.
  • Episode titles as shown in the player, so the comment thread can display a name.

Comments, likes, and display names are public: everyone using the extension on the same episode sees them.

What it does not collect

  • No account credentials, no email, no name beyond the display name you type.
  • No browsing history. The extension only runs on Hulu and Netflix player pages and only talks to the comment server.
  • No video or audio content. It reads the player's current time, not the stream.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Where data goes

Data is sent to and stored by the Community Swim server (hosted on Cloudflare). Requests include your IP address, which is used only for rate limiting and is not stored with comments. A cached copy of comments for episodes you've watched, your settings, and your anonymous identifier are kept in your browser's extension storage.

Your choices

  • Delete any comment you posted from the comment itself.
  • Reset your anonymous identifier from the extension's popup (Identity → Reset). This disconnects you from past comments; it does not delete them.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all local data.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13.
